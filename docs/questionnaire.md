# Survey Instrument

Outline of the structured security questionnaire used in this study. Items were rated on Likert-style or multiple-choice scales. This outline is provided for transparency and reproducibility; the exact deployed wording may differ.

## Section 1 — Demographics

- Age group
- Occupation
- Primary device used for payments
- Frequency of digital payments

## Section 2 — Gateway Usage

- Which payment gateways do you use? (Paytm / PayU / CCAvenue / other)
- Which gateway do you use most often?
- How long have you used digital payment gateways?

## Section 3 — Authentication

- Are you aware of two-factor authentication (2FA)?
- Do you enable 2FA when available?
- How much do biometric logins increase your trust in a payment app?
- Have you ever disabled a security feature for convenience?

## Section 4 — Encryption and Privacy

- How well do you understand how payment data is encrypted?
- How concerned are you about how your payment data is stored and shared?
- Do you check for security indicators (HTTPS, trust seals) before paying?

## Section 5 — Reliability and Failures

- How often do you experience slow transaction processing?
- How often do authentication attempts fail unexpectedly?
- Does a failed transaction reduce your trust in the gateway?

## Section 6 — Trust

Rate each gateway (Paytm, PayU, CCAvenue) on:

- Perceived security
- Perceived privacy
- Reliability
- Overall trust
- Likelihood to recommend

## Section 7 — Fraud and Recommendations

- Have you or someone you know experienced payment fraud?
- Which controls would most improve your trust? (biometric auth, tokenization, fraud alerts, clearer privacy policy)
- Open-ended: what should payment platforms improve?

## Scales

| Scale | Range |
|---|---|
| Agreement | 1 = Strongly disagree … 5 = Strongly agree |
| Frequency | 1 = Never … 5 = Very often |
| Trust | 1 = Very low … 5 = Very high |
